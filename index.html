d. Grammar Focus: Present Simple and Present Continuous

Present Simple

We use the present simple to talk about habits and routines.

I usually don't go out much during the week.

Form: I/you/we/they + verb; he/she/it + verb-s/es.

Present Continuous

We use the present continuous for actions happening now.

I'm getting ready to go out.

Form: am/is/are + verb-ing.

Compare: usually → present simple; now → present continuous.
